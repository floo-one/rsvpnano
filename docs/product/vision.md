# Product vision

A pocket reader that turns a spare 2–10 minutes into time spent reading, with little effort spent finding something. Preserve RSVP Nano's working RSVP/page-reading, offline SD library, hardware support and companion workflows.

The first user is the owner of a Waveshare LCD 3.49 rev2 with a mixed collection of books and RSS articles. The observed problem is hard-to-identify horizontal spines; the user reports increasing article volume. We have not measured selection time or interviewed other users. Do not present broader demand as validated.

Success is finding, opening and resuming content confidently. A pleasant library is the entry point, not a commitment to a new content platform. A future use worth exploring is a personally prepared travel or language-learning reading packet, entirely offline; it is outside the first release.

Principles: readable titles before decoration; consistent touch behavior; explicit unknown metadata; existing reading behavior preserved; no notification/feed engagement loops; no new persistent schema until justified.

The user selected **readable list first** on 2026-10-18. Detailed interaction and release acceptance remain proposed. See [requirements](requirements.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md) and [design plan](../plans/library-redesign.md).
